# v1.1.20

**Quill can now tell you when there's a newer build, and install it for you.**

## What changed

Until now the only way to learn a new Quill existed was for someone to mention it. Quill now
looks at the shared Drive folder your Mac already syncs, notices when the copy there is newer
than the one you're running, and offers to install it. It does this once when it starts, and
whenever you pick **Check for Updates** from the Help menu.

Installing takes a moment: Quill copies the new version in, closes, and reopens itself. Your
documents are saved first, exactly as they are when you quit normally, and an update will never
discard unsaved work. If anything goes wrong partway, your existing copy is put back.

**This still reaches your Mac the same way, and nothing new leaves it.** Quill reads a folder
already on your disk. It does not contact a server, has no account of its own, and sends no
telemetry. If you'd rather it didn't look at all, the update prompt has a checkbox to stop the
automatic check — Check for Updates keeps working when you want it.

If you don't see the Drive folder on your Mac, Quill stays quiet about it rather than nagging
you with an error you can't do anything about.

## Updating to this one

The usual way, one last time: quit Quill, drag **Quill.app** from the synced Drive folder into
**Applications** replacing the old copy, and open it again. From here on Quill can offer that
step itself. Your documents and settings carry over.
