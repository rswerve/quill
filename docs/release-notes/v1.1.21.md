# Quill v1.1.21

**The document now uses the whole window, and table columns are sized by what's in them.**

The document column was pinned at a fixed width no matter how large you made the window, so a wide screen was mostly empty background and anything with a wide table was cramped into the middle of it. The column now fills the space you give it, with a side margin that grows as the window does.

- **The page follows the window.** Narrow it and the text narrows with it; widen it and the text uses the room. If a line ever feels too long, narrowing the window or raising the zoom both shorten it — where before, nothing you could do would make the column wider.

- **Table columns are sized by their contents.** A column holding `1.00` no longer claims the same width as one holding two sentences. This shows up most in tables that mix a short label or a number with a paragraph, where the short column used to take an equal share and squeeze the text. Printing and PDF export keep the even split, which is what lets a wide table always fit the page.

No file-format or data changes. Documents, comments, suggestions, chat threads, and linked sessions carry over unchanged.

## Install

Inside Truss, Quill is shared as a ready-to-run app on the team's Google Drive — copy `Quill.app` from the shared drive (in Finder, from your synced folder) to your Applications folder and open it. The [README](../../README.md#installing-quill) has the details and the [User Guide](../USER_GUIDE.md) a full walkthrough. The Claude features require the [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) CLI on the same Mac.
